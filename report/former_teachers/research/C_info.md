# C. Information & nudges (agent report, 30 Oct 2026)

## NSW Teachers Re-Engage
- From Nov 2023 department contacted "more than 1500 teachers" who left in past five years; release 8 May 2024: "more than 260" interested; 145 linked with schools for casual/temporary work across >600 schools; "close to 150" set to return that term; "around 60%" coming out of retirement. Offer: simpler re-employment, help with accreditation, approval to teach, WWCC; permanent posts and small-group tutoring offered. Context: wage cap removed, "largest pay rise in decades", admin workload reduced. https://www.nsw.gov.au/media-releases/retired-teachers-return-to-classrooms
- Pay rise effective 9 Oct 2023: start salary $75,791 -> $85,000; top $113,042 -> $122,100. https://www.nsw.gov.au/media-releases/nsw-teachers-to-receive-largest-pay-rise-in-decades
- Vacancies 1,294 in Feb 2025, down 40% on 2023; peak 3,311 in 2022. https://www.nsw.gov.au/ministerial-releases/teacher-vacancies-drop-under-minns-labor-government
- Returner page (8 Jul 2026) no mention of Re-Engage. https://education.nsw.gov.au/teach-nsw/find-teaching-jobs/returning-teachers
- Press: targeted those resigned/retired 2019-2021; department paid $100 NESA fee 2024 for returning retirees (Cessnock Advertiser 12 Feb 2024).
- Statewide, not "that district". Status after May 2024 unknown.

## Lower Saxony letter & RLSB contact points
- Letter dated 1 Nov 2023, signed Kultusministerin Julia Willie Hamburg, to "angehende Pensionärinnen und Pensionäre / angehende Rentnerinnen und Rentner"; priority needs MINT, music, art, non-Gymnasium lower secondary, certain districts; many consider teaching "ein paar Stunden" but put off by "Bürokratie"; civil servants: defer retirement, withdraw application, direct fixed-term re-employment, volunteer; salaried: work beyond end date or simplified direct re-employment. https://www.mk.niedersachsen.de/download/201317/Schreiben_an_angehende_Pensionaerinnen_und_Pensionaere.pdf
- Landtag Drs. 19/8802 (answer 24 Oct 2025): school heads asked to approach proactively; RLSB (Braunschweig, Hannover, Lüneburg, Osnabrück) implement; "Ansprechpersonen stehen in jedem RLSB zur Verfügung"; simplified process: no new police certificate if immediate re-employment; no EiS-Online application for "direkte Anschlussbeschäftigung an der (ehemaligen) Stammschule"; funding each budget year, 2025 "deutlich erhöhte Mittel", further increase signalled for 2026; fixed-term staff previously permanent teachers past retirement age (1 Nov): 2020 229; 2021 257; 2022 362; 2023 401; 2024 437 (2024: GS 114, BBS 92); no data on expressions of interest or duration; earnings ceiling abolished from 1 Oct 2024 for those past statutory age (conflicts with press release 31 Oct 2024). https://www.landtag-niedersachsen.de/drucksachen/drucksachen_19_10000/08501-09000/19-08802.pdf
- Aug 2023: ministry wanted to write to pensioners but no longer held their personnel files -> letter went to those approaching retirement. https://www.news4teachers.de/2023/08/lehrermangel-ministerium-will-pensionaere-anschreiben-hat-aber-personalakten-nicht-mehr/
- "Weg der 1000 Schritte" official framing. https://www.mk.niedersachsen.de/startseite/aktuelles/start-ins-2-schulhalbjahr-2023-2024-unterrichtsversorgung-verbessert-sich-leicht-trotz-steigender-schulerzahlen-und-zusatzbedarfe-kultusministerin-hamburg-wir-werden-den-weg-der-1000-schritte-konsequent-weitergehen-229169.html
- "Welcome Centers" NOT confirmed for teachers: in Lower Saxony "Welcome Center" = centres for foreign skilled workers (Ministry of Social Affairs, 7 centres, ~EUR 1M). Official docs name RLSB contact persons. Ask Volkmar.
- Interview-only: contact ~6 months before retirement; part-time, specific subjects; not class teachers or formal mentors.

## Austria
- Vienna Bildungsdirektion contacted "nearly 300" retired teachers Dec 2022, 3 returned; late Feb 2023 all heads asked to check who could be kept beyond 65 via personal conversations; 46 Vienna teachers deferred retirement previous year, 20 so far in 2023. ORF Wien 29 Mar 2023 https://wien.orf.at/stories/3200562/
- National: parliamentary answer 12765/AB (18 Jan 2023, question 13105/J): 42 active provincial teachers whose new entry came after age 63 (26 at 65+), data Sep-Oct 2022 (before Vienna letters); UA 15, Vbg 6, Vienna 5, Sbg 6, LA 5, Tyrol 3, Styria 2, Bgld/Car 0. https://www.parlament.gv.at/dokument/XXVII/AB/12765/imfname_1504460.pdf
- ORF national 11 Mar 2023: Salzburg contacted all retired teachers up to 67; Vorarlberg sends re-entry info with pension application forms; Styria uses principals; federal schools ~30 returners. https://oesterreich.orf.at/stories/3198263/
- Ö1 13 Nov 2025: "etwa 30" retired teachers working nationwide; low entry salaries, high tax on extra income (press). https://oe1.orf.at/programm/20251113/813471/Mit-66-Jahren-noch-einmal-Junglehrer

## England
- School Workforce in England (4 Jun 2026, Nov 2025 data): returners FTE 2025/26 17,740 of 41,012 qualified entrants (43.3%); 2024/25 17,431 / 41,797 (41.7%); 2023/24 17,012 / 42,993 (39.6%); 2021/22 14,718; NQ entrants 2025/26 16,300 -> returners outnumber newly qualified. https://explore-education-statistics.service.gov.uk/find-statistics/school-workforce-in-england
- DfE 6,500 teachers delivery plan (CP 1508-II, Feb 2026): "Get support returning to teaching advisory service" + step-by-step guide; no usage figures. https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6995de6aa58a315dbe72bf7c/6500_additional_teachers_delivery_plan_print_ready_version.pdf
- Adviser service status after Feb 2026 unconfirmed; getintoteaching URL redirects to Teaching Vacancies guide. https://teaching-vacancies.service.gov.uk/jobseeker-guides/return-to-teaching-in-england/return-to-teaching
- "Come back to teaching" (20 Dec 2021 release): Omicron emergency call to recently retired and career changers to cover absences "even a day a week", sign-up via GIT, placement via supply agencies. https://www.gov.uk/government/news/education-secretary-calls-for-ex-teachers-to-return-to-classrooms ; press: 485 signed up (~10% of agencies' data) + 100 Teach First alumni (PoliticsHome 11 Jan 2022).
