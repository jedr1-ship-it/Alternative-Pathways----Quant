# A. Pension/earnings rules (agent report, 30 Oct 2026)

## Ireland
- Waiver of abatement under s.52(4) Public Service Pensions (Single Scheme and Other Provisions) Act 2012; Circular 0003/2021 -> 0004/2024 -> 0017/2026 (issued 26 Jan 2026, covers 1 Jan 2026-31 Dec 2027). Qualified teachers in capacity qualified, where need cannot practicably be met other than by a retired teacher; excludes retirees who have taken up a post extending beyond 50 days (permanent, CID); first 50 days in calendar year waived; any day counts as full day. https://www.asti.ie/document-library/circulars-00172026-extension-to-the-waiver-of-abatement-of/circular-0017-2026-extension-to-the-waiver.pdf
- Take-up unverified (">750" Sep-Oct 2021 snippet).

## Ontario
- OTPP 50 days per school year; currently no change. https://otpp.com/en-ca/members/preparing-to-retire/do-your-research-before-you-retire/learn-about-re-employment/
- If exceeded: work to end of month, pension suspended; resumes first month with no re-employment or 1 Sep. 95-day increase 2020-21, 2021-22, 2022-23: teacher/principal/VP roles, "positions that cannot otherwise be filled by qualified members"; monthly reporting to OTF and Ministry. https://www.otffeo.on.ca/en/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2020/11/50-Day-Re-employment-Rule-increased-to-95-Days-for-some-OTPP-retirees-until-June-30-2021.pdf
- 2022-23 OTF condition: Teacher Supply and Demand Action Table. 2023-24: OTF declined Ministry request (4 Mar 2024): >30,000 certified teachers not employed by boards; fear rule is "an incentive for some to take early retirement". https://www.otffeo.on.ca/en/news/otf-declines-ministry-of-educations-request-to-amend-50-day-re-employment-rule/
- No official take-up.

## France
- Code des pensions civiles et militaires L.84-L.86; full cumulation needs all pensions claimed and full rate; otherwise earnings capped at 1/3 of gross pension, excess deducted after allowance EUR 8,124.97 (2025); 2023 reform: full-rate retirees build new rights; LFSS 2026 reforms conditions (first pensions from 1 Jan 2027, unverified detail). Written question 11063 answered 12 May 2026. https://questions.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/17/questions/QANR5L17QE11063.pdf
- Q 2072 = higher-education vacataires (Higher Ed ministry, 28 Mar 2023): six-month gap applies only when returning to same/last employer. Q 7305 never answered.
- No take-up figures.

## Portugal
- DL 51/2024 Art. 5 "Contratação de docentes aposentados e reformados". Repealed by DL 175/2026 (3 Sep 2026) Art. 4 with effect 1 Sep 2026 ("deixou de se justificar"). https://www.madeira.gov.pt//Portals/10/Documentos/DL_175_2026%2C%20de%203%20de%20setembro.pdf
- EUR 750/month supplement survives for teachers eligible to retire who keep teaching in shortage zones (Grande Lisboa, Península de Setúbal, Algarve), Despacho 10024/2026.
- Take-up (press): 79 expressed interest; 62 provisionally valid (Público 5 Nov 2024); 55 validated on final lists for Jan 2025 start vs quota 200 (Observador 19 Dec 2024 https://observador.pt/2024/12/19/a-partir-de-janeiro-55-professores-que-estavam-reformados-vao-poder-voltar-a-dar-aulas/). FNE union: "pouco eficaz".

## Lower Saxony earnings ceiling
- Landtag Drs. 19/5338 (Sep 2024): ceiling 100% of pensionable pay; since 2019 +25% only from three years after statutory age; 1 Oct 2022-30 Sep 2024 raised to 150%; 2024 amendment § 64 NBeamtVG removes ceiling once age limit reached; full-time retiree could receive ~172% of pensionable pay. https://landtag-niedersachsen.de/drucksachen/drucksachen_19_07500/05001-05500/19-05338.pdf
- 2022: 9-10 lessons/week on average without cut (ministry). Abolition "Zum 31.10.2024 … gestrichen" (ministry 22 Jan 2025). Early retirees still capped (71.75% of ceiling + mini-job threshold).
- Deutsches Schulportal survey (2 Aug 2023): 13 of 16 Länder actively approach retirees; >5,000 stayed/returned nationwide (<1% of ~800,000); Lower Saxony 435 (~0.6%); NRW 1,304; Bavaria ~690; BW 531; Berlin 400; no age cap. https://deutsches-schulportal.de/bildungswesen/laenderueberblick-umfrage-lehrermangel-pensionierte-lehrer/

## Austria
- Resch, K. (2025), "Retired teachers return to school to tackle teacher shortages in Austrian schools. An untapped reserve or emergency response?", European Journal of Teacher Education, doi:10.1080/02619768.2025.2531930. Abstract: 14 interviews; terms negotiated individually with heads; finances, health, family, workplace; "None of the retired teachers in the study would repeat their return"; "not an adequate policy response". https://eera-ecer.de/ecer-programmes/conference/30/contribution/61244
- Union: returners get "das Gehalt eines Junglehrers" (Wiener Zeitung 23 Jan 2023).

## Michigan PA 147 of 2023
- Signed/effective 10 Oct 2023; until 10 Oct 2028; after six consecutive months retired (bona fide termination) any position, no earnings limit, no effect on pension/subsidy; before six months: $15,100/yr cap; superintendents excluded within six months. https://www.michigan.gov/orsschools/pa-147-of-2023-faqs
- Safer wording: shortened wait to six months, after which no earnings limit (cap removal may be PA 184 of 2022).

## New Mexico HB 73
- Laws 2022 ch. 20, effective 18 May 2022; return 90 days after retiring, up to 36 months, no earnings/FTE cap; non-refundable employee+employer contributions; no service credit. https://www.nmlegis.gov/handouts/ALESC%20120522%20Item%205%201%20-%20%20Educational%20Retirement%20Board%20Presentation.pdf
- Laws 2025 ch. 144 extended to 60 months; low-earnings option $15,000->$25,000. https://www.erb.nm.gov/rtw-comparison-table/
- ERB Oct 2022 table: ~74 in 36-month programme, 22 K-12 teachers (indicative).

## Louisiana
- Act 394 of 2024 (HB 967) -> superseded by Act 344 of 2026 (SB 14), effective 1 Jul 2026, single RTW law; 12-month wait; 65+ no pension impact; general cap 25%->50% of final average compensation; critical shortage incl. adult ed, any certified teacher; employer certifies shortage, advertises, <3 qualified non-retiree applicants; employer+employee contributions; actuarial report before 2034 session. https://www.trsl.org/retirees/return_to_work

## Georgia
- HB 385 (1 Jul 2022-30 Jun 2026): 30+ years, 1 year retired, full-time classroom in high-need area set by RESA; employer pays both contributions; no credit. SB 150 (effective 1 Jul 2026, sunset 30 Jun 2030): >=50% of schedule in qualifying subject (CTAE, maths, SPED, science, ELA/reading/writing with endorsement); each school system defines highest-need areas from 5-yr average with GaDOE/GaPSC. https://www.trsga.com/employer/hiring-trs-retirees/sb-150-faqs/
- Audit Report 24-20 (Apr 2025): 635 retirees FY2023-25, 241->428/yr, avg ~350 (<1% of ~125,000); 133 systems (55%) used it; 64% returned to same system; two-year retention 2023 cohort 43%; 56% of systems offered post to non-retiree first; TRS received $8.6M employers + $2.4M employees FY2023-24; 45 of 72 non-user systems wanted to use it but no eligible retirees. https://www.audits.ga.gov/ReportSearch/download/32615

## North Carolina 1999-2009 (Jarrold-Grapes & Mahler 2024, EPAA 32(4))
- Full-time return with salary + pension (part-time up to 50% of prior salary otherwise allowed); 1999 limited to low-performing schools as interim/substitute, lifted Jun 2000; set to expire 2003, extended, expired autumn 2009. ~3,500 teachers (2.1% of sample); 52% of schools hired >=1; peak <2% of teachers 2008; 54% returned to own school; 30% in poorest-quartile vs 21% least poor; 30% vs 18% Black share. Cannot show vacancy/achievement effects. Gain "as much as 50% more". https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ1425366.pdf

## Virginia
- Critical-shortage exception 2001 (Code § 51.1-155(B)(3)); 2022: pension unaffected, no accrual; from 1 Jul 2023 break 12->6 months, specialist support posts added, until 1 Jul 2028, employer contributions now required. VRS RD577 https://rga.lis.virginia.gov/Published/2023/RD577/PDF
- Take-up: 699 critical-shortage posts by retirees 2008-2022; 107 in 2022-23; 154 in 2023-24 (to 25 Oct 2023); Jul-Oct returns 18 (2022) -> 150 (2023), attributed to shorter break. 2007 report: cost-neutral.
- ED3 (1 Sep 2022): licences for retirees with lapsed licences; all vacant K-12 posts count as critical shortage (most already designated). VRS RD856 https://rga.lis.virginia.gov/Published/2022/RD856/PDF
