# E. Flexible positions & re-certification (agent report, 30 Oct 2026)

## Singapore
- FAJT: 1 day-10 weeks per appointment; teaching only or teaching + CCA/other duties; pro-rated leave after 90 days cumulative; NIE PGDE/DipEd; former MOE officers need good performance last 3 yrs; REMS 2.0 portal registration 3 yrs; schools contact registrants. Page 22 May 2026. https://www.moe.gov.sg/careers/adjunct-and-relief-schemes/flexi-adjunct-teaching-scheme
- Contract Adjunct Teaching Scheme: former MOE teachers only; 1-1.5 yr contracts renewed yearly, full or partial workload; no promotion/bonus. https://www.moe.gov.sg/careers/adjunct-and-relief-schemes/contract-adjunct-teaching-scheme
- "adjunct teachers programme" started Oct 2004 "to attract former trained teachers to rejoin the teaching profession… under more flexible terms" (MOE press release 29 Sep 2004).
- 2022-2024 avg ~220 engaged as FAJT within 12 months of leaving; tracking duration "not meaningful" (reply 23 Sep 2025). https://www.moe.gov.sg/news/parliamentary-replies/20250923-statistics-on-teachers-who-joined-flexi-adjunct-teaching-scheme-and-median-duration-of-teachers-on-scheme
- Resignation rate 2022-24 2.4%, 1.7% excluding those who joined FAJT (25 Sep 2025). https://www.moe.gov.sg/news/parliamentary-replies/20250925-number-of-teachers-who-have-entirely-left-teaching-profession-in-last-three-years
- Paid hourly in experience tiers, no bonuses; top tier <60% of median classroom-teacher package (9 Jan 2024). https://www.moe.gov.sg/news/parliamentary-replies/20240109-median-annual-salary-of-flex-adjunct-teachers-in-past-five-years
- Unconfirmed: 10 Sep 2026 answer: ~1/4 of resigning teachers engaged as FAJT within a year (party website only).

## Colorado Retired Mentors (REL 2017-225, DeCesare, McClelland & Randel 2017)
https://ies.ed.gov/sites/default/files/migrated/rel/regions/central/pdf/REL_2017225.pdf
- RCT Aurora Public Schools, 2013/14-2014/15, 77 teachers, 11 Title I elementary schools, within-school randomisation; probationary teachers (first 3 yrs in district); mentors recently retired district teachers and principals, >=5 yrs in district, USD 41/hour; ~1 h/week one-to-one + monthly cohort + quarterly principal meetings, 2 years; comparison: "buddy" ~15 h, USD 500 stipend.
- Maths yr1 ES 0.064 (p=.045, ~1 month learning); yr2 0.061 (p=.113 ns); reading 0.066 ns; evaluation ratings no effect; retention ns (11 programme vs 5 comparison left); exploratory: retention odds doubled per 10 extra mentoring hours; cost ~USD 171/student.

## Number Rockets (Gersten et al. 2015, AERJ 52(3):516-546)
- Cluster RCT 76 schools, 4 urban districts, 994 at-risk first graders; groups of 2-3, 40-min lessons, ~17 weeks, 45 lessons (~30 h); 86 tutors from local pools of retired and substitute teachers; 1 day training + 2x2h follow-ups; ES 0.34 on TEMA-3; WWC "meets standards with reservations". Tutors hired via nationwide temporary services company; 77.3% retired and/or substitute teachers; 44% had 0-5 yrs teaching experience. https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED529429.pdf

## AARP Experience Corps
- Over-50 volunteers tutor children to read by end of grade 3; 18 communities; 2023-24 1,284 volunteers, 3,659 children. https://www.aarp.org/experience-corps/
- Morrow-Howell et al. (2009), Washington University CSD: within-school randomisation 23 schools Boston/NY/Port Arthur; 881 students; significant gains passage comprehension and teacher-rated reading; ES .13-.17 for 35+ sessions; no benefit for IEP students. Volunteers older adults, not necessarily former teachers.

## Zaragoza 10 Mirrors
- Lizalde Gil & Cortés Pascual (2019); project PIPOUZ_17_216 Jan-Jun 2018; 10 retired teachers (4 early childhood, 6 primary) mentored 57 3rd/4th-year students; 3 sessions Mar-May 2018; attendance 75%; 37.5% said improved training, 56.25% helped orientation.

## Bahamas

Ministry release 17 Jul 2026: Reservist Program "uses the platform of its Supply Teacher Program"; 162 public schools on 24 islands; ~300 shortage. 23 Sep 2026: >100 Bahamian teachers recruited (returners, retirees, graduates combined), another ~100 in vetting. https://znsbahamas.com/teacher-recruitment-update/ ; no regulations/pay/reservist-specific figures.

## New Zealand
- Education and Training Act 2020 Sch. 3 cl. 10: Council may renew without satisfactory recent teaching experience (2 yrs in last 5) if applicant agrees to refresh process; Teaching Council Rules 2016 Part 9A (Amendment Rules 2021, in force 22 Oct 2021).
- Before Sep 2024 Return-to-Practice Plan (post-Covid) alongside TER. 13 Sep 2024 Minister short-term measures (relievers skip refresher; Limited Authority to Teach). Temporary light-touch RTPP replaced TER; extended to 31 Dec 2025 (unconfirmed detail).
- Fees for returners since Oct 2024; 352 supported (28 Apr 2025). Universal fee coverage 1 Jul 2025-30 Jun 2028, NZD 53.3M over three years (~40,000 beneficiaries yr1, 115,000 over three). https://workforce.education.govt.nz/budget-2025-fund-new-zealand-teacher-practising-certificates-0
- Template first April 2025, updated Dec 2025.
- 10 Dec 2025: Council renamed RTPP as Kia Maia, made permanent and expanded to some who would otherwise need TER, from 1 Jan 2026. https://teachingcouncil.nz/en/news-and-advocacy/news-and-articles/introducing-kia-maia
- Process: renew in My Rawa portal; if <2 yrs in last 5 and job offer -> Kia Maia plan (self-assessment vs Standards, >=3 resources per standard, first 12 months, co-developed and signed by principal; reviewed by Council staff, not competence test); certificate renewed; offer becomes firm. Without job offer TER "likely" still required; TER may be required after 5+ years of Kia Maia renewals. https://teachingcouncil.nz/en/maintain-my-practising-certificate/manage-my-practising-certificate/renew-after-time-away
- 20 half-days relief per calendar year for registered teachers without current certificate is standing allowance.
- Registration policy revision log says "from 1 January 2027" (probable typo).
- 501 Kia Maia users by 22 Oct 2026: personal communication (Ariane Madgwick, Registration Manager).
