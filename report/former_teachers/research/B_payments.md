# B. Direct payments (agent report, 30 Oct 2026) — key verified facts

## Quebec
- Cabinet memo 21 Sep 2020: retirees (retired <5 yrs) paid on single salary scale from first day of supply work; before: flat supply rate until 20th consecutive day. Cost est. CAD 4,545 per retiree per 20-day block. https://cdn-contenu.quebec.ca/cdn-contenu/gouvernement/MCE/dossiers-soumis-conseil-ministres/Memoire-Retour-des-enseignants-retraites.pdf
- Legal basis: 2020-21 Décret 964-2020 (health emergency); from 2021-22 yearly "ententes hors convention" CPNCF + Treasury Board + each union federation, filed under art. 72 Labour Code. FAE history table: https://www.seom.qc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/002-Instance-SEOM-AG-2025-09-24-Entente-pour-les-personnes-retraitees-revenant-a-lemploi.pdf ; signed 2024-25 agreement (27 Sep 2024): https://sehy.qc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Mesures-incitative-pour-le-personnel-enseignant-retraite-de-retour-au-travail-2024-2025.pdf
- Premiums: 2020-21 scale pay, no premium; 2021-22 to 2023-24 7.89% (employer RREGOP share) from 1 Oct 2021; 2024-25 7.5% supply/hourly + 12.5% for contracts >=3 months; 2025-26 7.5% removed, 12.5% for contracts >=1 month (FAE accepted "malgré le retrait de la prime salariale de 7,5 %"; retirees exempt from group insurance); 2026-27 draft extends 2025-26 terms to 30 Jun 2027 (signed version not found). https://www.seom.qc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/005-Instance-SEOM-AG-2026-06-16-Entente-pour-les-personnes-retraitees-revenant-a-lemploi.pdf
- Eligibility from 2024-25: any retiree of education network whatever retirement date/place, legally qualified; employers must first use qualified staff already employed (clause 13).
- NOT made permanent: 24 Jan 2022 release only wanted to make it lasting; 700 retirees attracted 2020-21; ~CAD 412/day vs usual max CAD 212.15. https://newswire.ca/fr/news-releases/operation-main-d-oeuvre-offensive-pour-attirer-davantage-de-personnel-dans-le-domaine-de-l-education-803384845.html
- 2022-23 funding CAD 13.3M; >700 retirees committed 2021-22 (23 Jun 2022). https://newswire.ca/fr/news-releases/plus-de-130-m-pour-contrer-la-penurie-de-main-d-oeuvre-en-education-892673222.html
- Measure 15178 (budget rules for school service centres), part 1 scale pay, part 2 premiums; withdrawn pending annual negotiation (12 Jun 2025 working doc), re-added 20 Jan 2026 for 2025-26 ending 30 Jun 2026; funding after the fact from end-of-year counts. https://cdn-contenu.quebec.ca/cdn-contenu/education/financement/Regles-budgetaires-littoral-25-26-amendee.pdf . 2026-27: folded into measure 1380 (allocations from national agreements), after-the-fact envelope added by Treasury Board once agreement signed. https://cdn-contenu.quebec.ca/cdn-contenu/education/financement/Regles-budgetaires-css-fonctionnement-26-27-amendee.pdf
- Gov page (19 Feb 2026): any retiree with teaching authorisation; scale pay from day one; nearly double usual max; full pension, no further contributions. https://www.quebec.ca/en/government/work-government/jobs-education/teaching-general-education-youth-sector-vocational-training-adult-education/return-work-retired-teacher
- Press: >2,900 retirees 2022-23, up to CAD 460/day (Radio-Canada 5 Jul 2023) https://ici.radio-canada.ca/nouvelle/1993748/prime-enseignants-retraite-quebec-penurie ; >5,000 in 2024-25 (Le Devoir, Drainville letter 25 Aug 2025) https://www.ledevoir.com/actualites/education/912921/quebec-espere-nouveau-attirer-milliers-enseignants-retraites-ecoles
- MEQ presentation 14 Oct 2026 (team's own): fits; nuance: 7.5% dropped in 2025-26 agreement; 2026-27 downward adjustment reflects it and decreasing use of retirees.

## China Silver-Age Lecturing Plan
- MoE+MoF 教师〔2018〕7号, signed 4 Jul 2018, published 19 Jul 2018: 10,000 over 2018-2020; first cohort autumn 2018 in 8 provinces; retired principals, teaching researchers, special-grade and backbone teachers, generally <=65; rural compulsory-education schools in poor/ethnic/border counties ("三区三州" priority); not own former school; >=1 school year, contract renewed yearly; pension/status unchanged; housing by county. http://www.moe.gov.cn/srcsite/A10/s7151/201807/t20180719_343448.html
- Funding CNY 20,000/person/year cost-shared (central pays in western provinces; 1:1 central/provincial in central provinces; eastern provinces pay alone); covers work subsidy, travel, accident insurance; provinces may top up.
- Planned recruits (plans, not counts): 2023 6,000; 2024 7,000; 2025 7,000; 2026 6,000 (2026 notice adds end-of-service assessment by host school + county follow-up). URLs: 2023 http://www.moe.gov.cn/srcsite/A10/s7151/202308/t20230808_1072900.html ; 2024 https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202408/content_6967278.htm ; 2025 http://www.moe.gov.cn/srcsite/A10/s7151/202509/t20250926_1415058.html ; 2026 http://www.moe.gov.cn/srcsite/A10/s7151/202609/t20260929_1452671.html
- 2023 National Silver-Age Teachers Action Plan 教师〔2023〕6号 (10 departments, 14 Jul 2023): ~120,000 silver-age teachers across all levels within ~3 years. http://www.moe.gov.cn/srcsite/A10/s7151/202308/t20230829_1076752.html
- Cumulative >20,000 primary/secondary retirees recruited 2018-mid-2023, +~1,000 university. https://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_xwfb/s5147/202309/t20230901_1077691.html
- No national actual counts per year, no independent evaluation.

## Netherlands grant (Regeling tegemoetkoming herintreders po)
- Stcrt 2017, 62002 (24 Oct 2017), in force 1 Nov 2017: flat EUR 2,500 per returner to school board for guidance and support costs; paid in one sum; unspent part may go to other activities; appointed from 1 Aug 2017; contract >=6 months; not working as primary teacher 12 consecutive months before; covers also qualified who never started; budget EUR 1.25M to 31 Dec 2018 (max 500), first come first served; stille reserve >=31,000 in primary. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2017-62002.html
- Stcrt 2019, 28510 (14 May 2019): EUR 300,000 2019, EUR 250,000 2020; applications to 31 Oct 2020; expiry 1 Jan 2022. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/stcrt-2019-28510.html
- Evaluation letter 10 Jul 2020, Kamerstuk 35300-VIII nr. 212: 274 returners subsidised Oct 2017-Jun 2020; boards said most would have been hired/supported anyway; contributed "in beperkte mate"; not extended. https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/kst-35300-VIII-212.html
- DUO 28 May 2026: stille reserve 34,000 primary, 64,000 secondary+vocational (1 Oct 2024); ~2,400 returners/yr primary (halved over 10 yrs), 1,500 secondary+vocational. https://duo.nl/organisatie/pers/stille-reserve-oud-leraren-in-het-onderwijs-afgelopen-10-jaar-afgenomen.jsp
- Somers, Groot, van Merode, ESB 28 Feb 2024: reserve >62,000; ~22% returning full-time would close shortage. de Vries, van Klaveren, Stolp, ESB 27 Jun 2025: most reserve already works in education/health. https://esb.nu/herintrede-leraren-uit-stille-reserve-beperkte-oplossing-voor-tekorten/
- Draft ref "Somers et al. 2024b, De stille reserve..." not found; use ESB 2024 or Somers, Flerck, Groot, van Merode (2024) https://instituutgak.nl/?p=7017
