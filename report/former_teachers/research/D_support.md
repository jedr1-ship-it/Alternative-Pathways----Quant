# D. Support & training (agent report, 30 Oct 2026)

## Sweden — Skolverket web course for returning teachers
Source: Skolverket (2020), Webbutbildning för återvändande lärare och förskollärare – redovisning av uppdrag, 9 Dec 2020, dnr 2019:01515. https://www.skolverket.se/download/18.70f8d1a017495c3cb593080/1608285624392/Pdf7659.pdf
- Assignment in Skolverket's 2017 regleringsbrev; report due 18 Dec 2020.
- Published Oct 2018 on learning platform; complete Feb 2019 (grading module added).
- Content: changes in steering documents since 2010 Education Act and 2011 curriculum reform; common modules (knowledge and values, health promotion, multilingual learning environment, digitalisation, systematic quality work); teacher track adds grading and school-working life; teacher and preschool-teacher versions.
- Format: self-study with tests, reflections, forum; structured 40-week pace or own pace; original fixed sequence + single certificate at end.
- Free; primary target teachers with qualifying degree.
- Participation Oct 2018–Apr 2020: 1,018 registered (532 teachers, 486 preschool teachers); 175 completed >=1 module; 38 completed whole course; 17 downloaded certificate. ~1/3 of survey respondents were actual returners. Survey response 25%.
- Cost SEK 3,022,000 over 2017-19 = SEK 17,268 per participant (vs SEK 863 for comparable web course).
- Verdict: "Utbildningen har inte nått ut till målgruppen"; "långt ifrån ett tillfredställande resultat". Enrolment on own initiative; no employed respondent got working time, a mentor or group study from principal.
- Context: just over 3,000 teachers return each year outside preschool/leisure-time; >7,000 returned in 2019 across all school types (~3% of workforce); of returners 65+ (outside preschool) nearly 90% from retirement.
- Planned fixes Dec 2020: communication plan, outreach via principals' programme, unlock modules.
- Status 2026: deck URLs 404; no Skolverket page after 2020 found.

## England — Return to Teaching pilot 2015-16 (NFER, Buchanan et al. 2018)
https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5b33aeef40f0b67f6744de9d/Evaluation_of_the_Return_to_Teaching_pilot_programme.pdf
- NCTL; 60 school partnerships (School Direct) delivering in-school support from Jan 2016; EBacc subjects in secondary; subject knowledge, updating on practice/behaviour/assessment, classroom experience, mentoring. Grants £586,900 for 412 starters (£1,425/participant); free to returners.
- Targets 3,000 registrations / 650 returners. Result: 5,729 registered, 354 supported, 27 (8%) in permanent EBacc posts in state secondaries Nov 2016; 62 (18%) in any state-school post vs ~4% of non-supported registrants (selection caveat). 40% of employed returners part-time vs 28% of all returners. Cost per additional returner £37,400 (~cost of training a new teacher); "unlikely to represent good value for money".
- Lessons: timing (Jan-Oct, few posts by Sep); continuing support needs; limited part-time work (half of returners/schools said returners sought part-time; barrier for parents of young children); schools reluctant to offer teaching opportunities ("need to establish the quality of returners and concerns about possible disruption to lessons"); only 44% felt needs met (lack of practical teaching); barriers: lack of recent experience, workload, lack of flexible/part-time jobs, confidence; career-breakers double return rate. Recommendations: start earlier, target the "ready", mandatory classroom practice, tie to vacancies, online modules for common content, expect part-time.
- Separate adviser strand maths/physics 2015-16: 2 advisers, 541 eligible returners, 63 (11.6%) in state-school jobs by Sep 2016 (target 50). Straw et al. 2017 https://dera.ioe.ac.uk/29564/1/Maths_and_physics_teacher_supply_package-report.pdf ; Commons Library CBP-7222: conversion low "partly due to schools being reluctant to offer shadowing and placement opportunities and/or to employ returners".
- Returners Engagement Programme: published 18 Nov 2016, withdrawn 14 Mar 2018; NW and SE; maths, physics, MFL; lead schools bid for identified vacancies; 2-4 week course incl. teaching experience; referral by RtT advisers; funding £2,000 start-up per partnership, £1,500 per returner after employment, bursary up to £600 (£150/week) for childcare/travel; SE trial up to £1,500 extra per part-time returner. No evaluation. https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/returners-engagement-programme

## Victoria TREI design
- vic.gov.au (22 Sep 2026): "paid volunteer placement for up to 40 days"; away >=12 months; "Through mentoring from current staff, participants observe the class and school environment"; schools host for AUD 330/day covering paid placement and admin. Process map: TREI team matches by subject/experience/location/availability; participant agreement (up to 40 days, honorarium + admin fees); school pays invoice before placement; pay via eduPay; TREI records "re-engagement status" at end. https://content.sdp.education.vic.gov.au/media/trei-process-flow-3525

## Netherlands grant design
- Explanatory note: support "'opfrissen' van de kennis en vaardigheden", e.g. internal/external coach or refresher course; "niet bedoeld als toelage op het salaris"; cites 1998-2002 "Terug voor de klas" project. ~100 returners in 2018 (coach or digiboard training), ~200 by 20 Dec 2019 (https://zoek.officielebekendmakingen.nl/ah-tk-20192020-1201.pdf); 274 by Jun 2020 (evaluation letter). Regions offer bijscholing and coaching to returners.

## Evidence
- No causal evaluation of refresher/re-entry support on returns or retention found. NFER strongest (supported vs non-supported, selection bias). Larsen & Brandenburg (2026), "Former teachers who return to the profession: coming back on their terms", Teachers and Teaching, DOI 10.1080/13540602.2026.2700748 (qualitative; induction for new graduates "not relevant" to returners — snippet only).
- Retention of returners after re-entry not measured in any programme.
