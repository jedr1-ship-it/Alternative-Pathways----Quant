# OECD style rules applied to Section 3

Sources: OECD (2025), *OECD Style Guide (Fourth Edition)*, https://doi.org/10.1787/2ca15c7e-en
(rules and conventions); OECD (2015), *OECD Style Guide: Third Edition*, chapters
"Writing for the OECD" and "Drafting tips and principles of style" (writing guidance,
which the fourth edition moved to an internal OECD portal).

## Writing
- Inverted pyramid: main findings first; no generic openings ("This section aims to...").
- Main idea at the start of each paragraph; one idea per paragraph.
- Sentences of 15-20 words on average; active voice; plain English.
- Boxes: ancillary case studies, self-contained (beginning, middle, end), no longer than
  one printed page, each with its own "Source:" line.
- Avoid: "scheme" (use programme, plan, initiative), "address", "implement" (overused),
  "drive", "deliver", "foster", "leverage", "impact" as a verb, "respectively",
  "quite/rather/somewhat", "and/or", "firstly/secondly", "presently".

## Conventions
- British spelling with -ise endings; licence (noun), license (verb); programme.
- No Oxford comma; no em dashes; no contractions.
- Numbers: one to ten in words, 11 and above in figures; figures for all when mixed in
  context, and with %, currencies and units; hard space in numbers of four or more digits
  (5 000); "%" in text.
- Currencies: ISO code before the amount with a hard space (EUR 2 500, CAD 412).
- Dates: 1 October 2021 (hard space between day and month); ranges 2018-2020;
  school years 2020/21.
- Countries: "the United States", "the Netherlands", "the United Kingdom"; US as adjective;
  alphabetical order in lists.
- Titles of boxes, tables and headings in sentence case; capital after a colon.
- Sources under every table and box; never "Source: Authors" or "Source: OECD".

## References
- Author-date in text; up to three authors listed, four or more "et al.".
- Reports and journals in italics; articles, web pages and papers in roman within
  double quotation marks; legislation in roman.
- Web pages and press: URL plus "(accessed on Day Month Year)".
- Unpublished data: Author (unpublished), "Title".
