# Shared introduction

The same opening for every interview script. Read it out or paste it into the
invitation; it is three ideas and nothing more.

---

We are working on a chapter about former teachers for an OECD project on the
diversity of routes into the teaching profession. We are gathering the policies
and the experiences that countries have used to bring former teachers back to
the classroom — teachers who retired, teachers who moved into another sector,
and teachers who left work altogether.

What we are after is not the design on paper, which we can read, but what
actually happened: what worked, what did not, and what the people who ran these
policies would tell a country thinking of doing the same.

The third part of our work is about the teachers themselves. We are
characterising why teachers leave, and we want to see whether the policies that
try to bring them back speak to those reasons or to something else.

This conversation is to understand your experience. There are no right answers,
and anything you cannot discuss, we simply leave aside.
