# Netherlands
## Grant to schools for re-hiring former teachers, 2017–2022

**Who to ask:** the primary-education directorate of the Ministry of Education,
Culture and Science, and the agency that handled the applications (DUS-I).

**What we know:** The Ministry of Education paid primary schools up to 2,500
euros for every qualified teacher they re-hired who had been out of primary
teaching for at least a year, on a contract of six months or more. The money
went to the school board, not the teacher, and was meant for the guidance and
support a returner needs in the first months back. The scheme opened in November
2017 with a budget of 1.25 million euros, enough for about 500 returners, was
extended in 2019, took applications until November 2020 and expired in January
2022. In mid-2019 the ministry reported that more than a hundred returners had
used it, against a pool of qualified teachers outside education that it put at
more than 30,000. A Dutch teaching qualification does not expire, so there was
no re-licensing step on the way back. What we do not know is how much of the
money was finally used, whether the returners stayed, why the scheme was allowed
to lapse, and whether it was ever evaluated.

1. **In what context did the scheme come about in 2017, and with what purpose?**
   Why pay the school rather than the teacher?
   - Was that debated? What was the alternative?

2. **What did a school have to do to claim the grant, and what was the money
   spent on?**
   - Did 2,500 euros change a hiring decision, or did schools hire first and
     claim afterwards?

3. **According to the figures we have seen, much less of the money was used than
   was available. Is that right, and why?**
   - Was the scheme known to schools?
   - Did the six-month contract or the twelve-month absence rule get in the
     way?

4. **We understand the scheme was extended in 2019 and then allowed to lapse in
   2022. How were those decisions taken?**
   - Was it evaluated?
   - Did the work move to the regional partnerships, and does anyone there
     still target returners?

5. **Can you describe the profile of the teachers who came back through the
   scheme?** (e.g. people who had left for another sector, people who qualified
   and never started)
   - Why had they left primary teaching, and what made returning possible?

6. **We noticed that the number of returners in primary education has halved in
   a decade while the shortage persisted. How do you read that?**
   - Primary teachers leave over workload, class size and leadership. Does a
     grant to the school reach any of that?

7. **Is there any record of whether the teachers who came back through the
   scheme stayed?**

8. **Do you know about any other policy, in the Netherlands or elsewhere, that
   aims to re-attract former teachers?**

9. **Overall, what is your assessment of the scheme, and what has it taught
   you?**

10. **Is there someone at the ministry, or at the agency that handled the
    applications, you would recommend we speak to?**
