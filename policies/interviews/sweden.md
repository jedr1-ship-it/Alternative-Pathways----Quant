# Sweden
## Online course for returning teachers, since 2018

**Who to ask:** the National Agency for Education (Skolverket), the unit that
runs the course on its learning platform; and, for the origin of the measure,
the Ministry of Education, which commissioned it.

**What we know:** The National Agency for Education put a free online course on
its learning platform for qualified teachers and preschool teachers coming back
after working elsewhere. The returner logs in, picks the school or the preschool
track and takes only the modules they need, in any order, with a certificate for
each one. The modules cover the curricula, laws and official guidelines that
changed while they were away, which is what a teacher who has been out for years
is least sure about. It was built at the government's request, has been
available since October 2018, and is open to anyone with a Swedish teaching
licence or a qualifying degree. As far as we know the course is not a
requirement to return, since a Swedish teaching licence does not expire (to
confirm). What we do not know is how many teachers have taken it, whether they
went on to teach, and whether the agency has assessed it.

---

1. **In what context did the government commission this course, and with what
   purpose?** Did Sweden face strong teacher shortages at that moment?

2. **We would like to understand what kind of measure this is. Is the course a
   requirement for a teacher to return to the classroom, or is it voluntary?**
   - If it is voluntary, was there ever a requirement of this kind that the
     course replaced?
   - Do universities or private providers offer similar courses for returning
     teachers, and if so, what does the agency's course add: that it is free,
     that it is official, that it is online?

3. **How would you describe the course as a teacher experiences it: what they
   find when they log in, how long it takes, and what the certificate is for?**
   - Do schools or employers ask for the certificate, or is it only for the
     teacher?

4. **Who takes the course? Can you describe the profile of the teachers who
   register?**
   - Teachers who left for another sector, people who never started, retired
     teachers?
   - How long had they typically been away?

5. **Is there any record of how many teachers have taken the course since 2018,
   how many finished it, and how many went on to teach?**

6. **We understand the course was built to update returners on what changed
   while they were away. In your experience, is that what holds returning
   teachers back, or is it something else: confidence, workload, the hiring
   process?**

7. **Has the agency assessed the course, and what did it conclude?** We know of
   a report to the government in 2020.
   - Has the course been updated since, and is it still promoted?

8. **Do you know about any other policy, in Sweden or elsewhere, that aims to
   re-attract former teachers?**

9. **Is there someone at the agency, or at the ministry, you would recommend we
   speak to?**
