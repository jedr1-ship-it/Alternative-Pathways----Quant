# Australia — Victoria
## Teacher Re-Engagement Initiative, since 2022

**Who to ask:** someone in the Victorian Department of Education.

**What we know:** The Teacher Re-Engagement Initiative, running since late 2022,
offers switchers a paid placement of up to forty days in a government school
before they apply for a job. The re-engagement team calls the teachers that
registered on the program website; coaching is offered on applications and CV
preparation, and a placement is arranged with a serving teacher alongside.
Returners from a career break or retirement receive an honorarium of up to
12,000 dollars, while teachers back from more than two years of parental absence
resume duty temporarily on their own salary. More than 1,100 teachers registered
interest in the first months, and the 2023/24 State Budget put 12 million
dollars into placements to be completed by June 2025. What we do not know is how
many placements were made and how many led to a post, since no data has been
published.

   the school rather than centrally. Is that right, and how did that change come

   - Did schools' willingness to host change once they were paying?

   switchers)
   - Which of the program instruments was the most attractive for participants:
     the forty paid days, the coaching, the mentor or the route into a school?

7. **Is there any record of how well the initiative has worked: placements made,
   how many led to a post, an internal evaluation?**
   - What did it cost against what it produced?

8. **Overall, what is your assessment of its impact, and what would you change
   if you were designing it again?**
