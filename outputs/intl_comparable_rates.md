Every retained bar is an annual flow over the teacher stock with retirements included, 2021 or later. Definition deviations are labeled on each bar.

## Dropped, and why

- South Korea 2.1 (2024): excludes statutory-age retirements and private schools; not the same object. Official all-cause rates exist only through 2020 (elem 1.7 / middle 3.6 / high 3.7).
- Switzerland 8.4: latest national figure is 2010/11.
- Iceland 12.4-14.3 (2024): counts parental and other leave as attrition; compulsory schools only.
- Singapore ~2-2.4: resignations only, retirements excluded.
- Chile ~4.5: official study excludes retirement and death, two-year-out window.
- Scotland ~5.2: FOI-only modelling number, latest 2018, contaminated by career breaks.
- Norway 6.2-7.4 (2023/24): municipal-sector employer turnover (KS PAI), not profession exit; no official rate.
- Sweden, Finland, Denmark, Spain, Portugal, Canada, Taiwan, Russia: no usable official annual flow.

Compiled from four parallel research passes over primary sources; full per-country audits in the session research notes.
