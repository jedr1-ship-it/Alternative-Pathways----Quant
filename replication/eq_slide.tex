\begin{equation*}
\Pr\left(\text{leave}_{it}=1\right)\;=\;\Phi\!\left(\alpha
\;+\;\beta_{1}\,\text{Age}_{it}\;+\;\beta_{2}\,\text{Age}_{it}^{2}
\;+\;X_{it}'\gamma\;+\;\delta_{t}\right)
\end{equation*}

\vspace{16pt}

\noindent where $\text{leave}_{it}$ equals one if teacher $i$, who taught
during calendar year $t$, is no longer teaching at the following March
interview, whether employed in another occupation, unemployed, or out of
the labor force; $X_{it}$ collects indicators for female, Black, married,
separated or divorced, a master's degree, public school, pension coverage,
part-time hours, and full-year work, together with the number of own
children, the presence of a child under six, and a new baby during the
year; and $\delta_{t}$ are year fixed effects. The model is estimated by
maximum likelihood and average marginal effects are reported.

\vspace{12pt}

\noindent Sample: college-graduate teachers aged 18 and over, teaching
years 2015--2024; $n = 28{,}932$.
